Privacy Policy

Last updated November 8, 2026

In short: SongSet does not collect any information about you. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. Your songs stay on your devices and in your own iCloud account, which we cannot see.

What SongSet stores, and where

Your songs, services, keys and markings are saved on your iPad or iPhone. If iCloud is on, SongSet also keeps them in your own iCloud account using Apple's CloudKit, so your other devices stay up to date. That data belongs to your Apple account. We, the developer, have no access to it. Apple's privacy policy applies to iCloud.

Teams

When you start a team, its songs and services are shared through iCloud with the people who join. Anyone who has the team's code, QR code or invite link can join, so share them only with your team. The person who started the team can make a new code at any time, which stops the old one from working.

To let others find a team by its code, SongSet saves the code and the team's iCloud share link in Apple's public CloudKit database. It contains no names, songs or other personal information. When the team is deleted, this entry is removed.

SongSet does not ask for anyone's name. Each screen only picks a role: Singer, Band or Leader.

Following the leader

When a leader moves to the next song, nearby screens on the same Wi-Fi follow. This uses the local network (Apple's Bonjour) and goes directly from device to device. The messages say only which song and place to show, and they are signed with the team's key so other screens in the building cannot change what your team sees. Nothing is sent to us.

Camera and photos

The camera is used to scan a team's QR code and to read a song from a photo of a song sheet. The text is read on your device. The photo is not saved and is not sent anywhere.

Adding songs from a link

When you add a song from a web link, SongSet downloads that page straight from the website to your device to read the song. We do not see the links you use.

Children

SongSet does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Deleting your data

Leave a team from the Team screen. If you started the team, Delete the team removes it and its shared songs for everyone. Deleting the app removes the songs from that device. You can remove SongSet's iCloud data in Settings > your name > iCloud > Manage Account Storage.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
