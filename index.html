SongSet

Your worship team's songs, ready on every iPad and iPhone. Words, chords, keys and markings stay the same for everyone, with or without internet.

  • Join your team with a code, a QR code or a link.
  • Add songs from a web link, by pasting the words, from a photo of a song sheet, or from OnSong.
  • Change the key with one tap. Chords move with it.
  • Draw, highlight and mark repeats. The whole team sees them.
  • Plan a service, and lead it: every screen in the room follows the leader to the next song.

Support

Questions, a song that won't import, or something that doesn't look right? Email vlad@light3capital.com. We usually answer within a day.

How do I add my team?

One person taps Start a team. On the Team screen they show the QR code, read out the code, or send the invite link. Everyone else taps Join a team. Joining gives access to the whole team library.

Do I need an account?

No. SongSet uses the iCloud account already on your iPad or iPhone. To share a team, iCloud must be turned on in Settings.

Does it work without internet?

Yes. Songs and services are kept on the device. Changes sync the next time the device is online. Following the leader works over the room's Wi-Fi.

A web link didn't import.

Some sites don't share their song text. Copy the words on the page and use Paste the words instead. Chords in square brackets, like [G]Amazing grace, are kept.

How do I move my songs from OnSong?

In OnSong, export your songs or a backup and save the file to the Files app. In SongSet, open Team, then Settings, tap Import from OnSong and pick the file. Your sets come over too.

Who can change a song?

Everyone on the team. Changes ask to be confirmed first, so nothing changes by accident.

How do I leave or delete a team?

Open the Team screen and tap Leave the team. If you started the team, the button says Delete the team, and it deletes the team for everyone.

Song licenses

SongSet is a tool for your own charts. It does not include songs. Your church is responsible for having the right license (for example CCLI) for the songs it uses.
